## Findings
- No E4 or E8 variant passes the gate on the chase head against either base. The time-varying balls-left and wickets terms push the early-chase mean p lower (tvBW+rrr: 0.311-0.313 at 161-180), so they worsen the very bias they were meant to fix, and tvB is significantly worse overall (+0.0022 / +0.0023).
- rrr, rrrW and the recalibrations move the early-chase mean p by at most 0.004 and the overall loss by at most 0.0007, with CIs spanning 0. Platt picked lambda 0 (identity) in every fit, and phaseT scales are within 1.00-1.09 of 1.
- In this run k48 already has the higher early-chase mean p at 161-180 (0.372) than the baseline (0.361). Both still under-predict the actual 0.450 by 0.08-0.09 in every target band. The earlier E1 figures (baseline 0.405, k48 0.366) were not reproduced here: this run's baseline fit differs from E1 (chase log-loss 0.36838 vs 0.37192), so compare bands only within this run.
- First innings: tvB passes with a latest-season gain and an ECE improvement; tvW also passes but its latest-season dLL is +0.0017 and its ECE rises. phaseT is below the gain threshold, and tvB+phaseT equals tvB.

## Recommendation
- Chase head: adopt k48 as is (no E4/E8 add-on); no variant passes the gate and none removes the early-chase under-prediction, so that bias remains open.
- First innings: adopt baseline + tvB (balls-left time-varying terms); skip E8 recalibration.
