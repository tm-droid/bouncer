## New findings
Severity: bug / drift / leak / test-gap.

- N1 leak: scheduledDays comes from info.dates.length (days PLAYED). T/src/corpus.js:94, used at T/src/training.js:549 and tools/winprob-lab.mjs longRowsWide. Fails: a Test finished in 3 days gets scheduledDays=3, so the budget (3*540) reveals the result; serving uses 4-5 (M/src/winprob.js:724).
- N2 bug: ballsBowled++ on every delivery, wides and no-balls included. T/src/training.js:516; same in tools/winprob-lab.mjs:411-499. Fails: over with 2 wides counts 8 balls, so balls-remaining is understated vs serving (overs-derived).
- N3 bug: D/L targets and reduced budgets ignored in chase rows. T/src/build.js:170-209 (target = first total + 1, match.ballsBudget); corpus.js:99 stores method but never uses it. Fails: 20-over match cut to 5 overs with a D/L target of 70 is labelled as a normal 20-over chase.

- N5 bug: surnameLettersFor pairs squads with teams by position. M/src/context.js:341-352. Fails: if the squads array order differs from teams order, side A's players are matched against side B's surnames.
- N6 leak: followedOn computed from the whole match, not as of the ball. T/src/training.js:479-480, 543; tools/winprob-lab.mjs longRowsWide. Fails: first-innings rows of a match that later ends in a follow-on carry followedOn=1.
- N7 drift: lab reimplements teamFeatures, familiarity, hasVenue. tools/winprob-lab.mjs:221-238. Fails: lab metrics diverge from the trainer if either changes.
- N8 leak: lab strength term includes own match (comment at tools/winprob-lab.mjs:100-103); lab counters include own match (552-564). Lab scores are optimistic.
- N9 test-gap: train-win-probability/test/training.test.js and context.test.js mirror builder code instead of pinning values; no test for scheduledDays, wides, D/L, or follow-on cases.
- N10 leak: Test win-rate counters computed over the train window including the row's own match. T/src/build.js:533-544.
- N11 drift: prematchRowWide has post-toss columns (toss, firstBatsFirst). tools/winprob-lab.mjs:501-521. Fails if used on a pre-toss serving path.
- N12 bug (silent fallback): tools/winprob-live-score.mjs:370 `.catch(()=>null)` on wci downloads; a failed download scores as no data rather than failing.
- N13 drift: M/src/winprob.js:172 `round(oversRemaining*6)` treats the overs decimal as base-10 tenths-of-six; 79.2 style (overs.balls) inputs break. Fails: oversRem 12.3 means 12 overs 3 balls, not 12.3*6=74.
