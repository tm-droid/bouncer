## Hypotheses
1. Stale `modelAsOf` team terms: CONFIRMED, the dominant cause. Lagging the trainer to `modelAsOf` removes 91% of the elo diff, 95% of form, 82% of chase advantage, 100% of h2h. Code: `match-sync/src/context.js` buildContext 177-258 reads `teamStats`, h2h and ground tables of `model.asOf` (e.g. 2026-10-07 for a 2026-10-22 match), while training reads state up to the match date.
2. Known-both-sides neutralisation (`sideContextFor`, context.js 288-315): RULED OUT as a source. Serving team `known` is 99.55% (444/446); trainer team games>0 is 99.78%. It almost never fires.
3. Ground / XI joins: PARTLY CONFIRMED.
   - Ground: serving `venue.known` 77.1% against trainer `hasVenue` 90.6% (exact). After lagging, hasVenue still differs by 0.094 and familiarityDiff by 0.058 (58 sides >0.05). `pickGround(tables.venues[venueName(name)], city)` (context.js) returns null when city does not match and several grounds share the name; `visits` is keyed `key|gender`. Residual venue terms are below 0.05 mean but familiarity and hasVenue exceed it.
   - XI: serving carries an eleven for 37.7 points fewer sides than a complete Cricsheet eleven (168 of 446 sides differ). Caveat: my trainer side uses full elevens, not `thinnedElevens` (build.js 135), so this overstates; the trainer thins at JOIN_FAILURE rates, so the real gap is the difference between those rates and the live join rate. Not measured here.

## Causes for |diff| > 0.05
- eloDiff, formDiff, winRateDiff, h2hAdvantage, venueScoring, venueChaseAdvantage: model-table lag (hypothesis 1). Example 151273: formDiff serving 0.522 vs trainer 0.731.
- familiarityDiff, hasVenue: lag plus unmatched ground (hypothesis 3). Example 155551: serving hasVenue false, trainer true.
- XI presence: live join failures (`joinEleven` needs >=9 players and >=7 joined).

## Proposed fixes (not applied)
1. Refresh the published tables more often, or have the model carry per-team deltas to the match date; the lag of up to 15+ days is the biggest term. Measure how often the trainer refit runs against live matches.
2. Log which ground lookups returned null (name vs city) for the 23% without a venue, and make `pickGround` fall back to the name alone when the city mismatches; check `visits` key construction.
3. Re-run this script with the trainer side using `thinnedElevens` to quantify the real XI gap, and add the model fetch to compare pre-match probabilities.
4. Investigate the 110 unjoined matches (domestic/other competitions?) before concluding anything about them.
