The "You're offline" banner now follows the connection your phone is actually using. It used to
watch every network at once, so it could stay up while you were online, or miss a drop while you
were not.

Behind the scenes: the build's own checks were failing silently, which meant real problems could
reach a release unnoticed. Those checks pass again, the app's dependencies have been brought up to
date, and an advertising ID permission the app never used has been removed.
